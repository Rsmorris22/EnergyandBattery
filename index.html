Your rate can change without you lifting a finger.

Not a Think Energy thing — a Texas thing. Worth knowing no matter who you're with.

  • Plans expire quietlyNo action when your fixed term ends, and you're rolled onto a variable rate your provider sets.
  • Checking is freeTexas rules let you compare and switch penalty-free once your term is up.
  • Your power never skips a beatSame utility, same wires, same outage response — only the biller changes.
